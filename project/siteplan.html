Infinite Store • Site Plan

T-shirt sublimation & Customization

Overview

Site Name

Name: Infinite Store

Reasoning: I chose this name because sublimation offers "infinite" possibilities for design. Unlike other methods limited by colors, our service allows customers to print any photo, gradient, or complex art they imagine without limits.

Purpose

This website is both informative and interactive, showcasing the services offered, examples of completed work, a quote option, and more. It also offers customization of t-shirts, uniforms, and other garments using sublimation, DTF, and screen printing.

Scenarios

  • "I need 50 uniforms for my soccer team. Which technique is cheaper and more durable: DTF or Sublimation?"
  • "I have a photo of my dog. Can I print it on a black cotton shirt, or does it have to be white polyester?"
